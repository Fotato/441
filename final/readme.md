# Instructions

Move with arrow keys or WASD
* To move forward: Up Arrow Or W
* To move backward: Down Arrow or S
* To rotate clockwise: Right Arrow or D
* To rotate counterclockwise: Left Arrow or A
* To Shoot: Press or hold Spacebar

Avoid the red enemies and collect the flashing collectables to upgrade your tank!
